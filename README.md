**Community-reported codes.** The codes circulating on coupon trackers and creator posts. Some work, some don't, and the ones that do often carry exclusions that aren't obvious until you hit the checkout.

## How to apply a code without wasting a checkout

The mechanics are simpler than the coupon pages make them sound.

1. Add the items you want to your bag first. Most codes exclude sale merchandise, footwear, leather and suede goods, and third-party accessories, so a cart full of discounted items is the fastest way to see "code not valid."
2. Go to your Shopping Bag and find the box under "Promos" that says Enter Promo Code. Paste the code and hit Apply.
3. Check that the discount landed on every eligible item, not just one line. Partial application is common with restricted codes.
4. Note the shipping threshold before you finalize. Rewards members get free shipping on orders over $50, and cardmembers over $35. Paying $8 in shipping to save $9 with a code is a wash.
5. Keep the confirmation email with the code visible. If the price drops within the adjustment window, you'll want proof of what you paid.

Codes aren't case-sensitive, so don't retype it five times hoping capital letters change the outcome. If it doesn't apply, it's an eligibility problem, not a typing problem.

## Sale sections to check before you buy anything at full price

The clearance architecture is where Athleta hides its best prices, and the sections behave differently.

**We Made Too Much** is the main markdown section, and it's deepest on summer activewear, swim and running gear in mid-to-late July, then again in the back-to-school and January windows. **Last Chance** is the final markdown tier, and these are final-sale prices. You generally can't return them, so this is a section for rebuys and items where you already know your size.

**Price adjustments.** If something you bought drops in price, you can typically request a one-time adjustment within 14 days of the order date. This is why keeping your confirmation email matters. Some items bought with certain promotions are excluded, and you'll need proof of purchase.

One more practical note: Athleta ships within the United States, and coupon codes tied to partner memberships are U.S.-only. If you're shopping from abroad through a forwarding service, expect most promo codes to reject at checkout.

## Why the code you found didn't work

Almost every failure comes down to one of these:

- The code expired weeks ago and the aggregator never updated the page.
- It only applies to full-price merchandise, and your cart is all sale items.
- It excludes footwear, leather, suede or third-party accessories.
- It's a first-purchase-only code and your account has an order history.
- It's restricted to cardholders or a partner membership.
- It can't combine with the sitewide promotion that's currently running.

If the code is old but the sale is live, the sale is usually the better deal anyway.
